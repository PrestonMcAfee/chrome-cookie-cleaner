Privacy Policy
Effective Date: October 30, 2026

Tab-Close Cookie Cleaner was developed with a strict privacy-first principle:

No Data Collection or Transmission: The extension operates entirely offline on your local machine. It does not collect, log, track, or monitor any browsing history, personal data, credentials, or analytical telemetry.

No Remote Servers: The extension maintains zero communication with external servers, tracking services, or third-party APIs.

Storage Usage: The chrome.storage API is used exclusively to store your personal domain whitelist and is synchronized strictly within your own Google account via Chrome's native sync engine.

Data Sale & Transfer: No data is ever sold, transferred, monetized, or shared with third parties.
