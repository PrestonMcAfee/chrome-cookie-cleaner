Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 31, 2026

100% Local Execution: All evaluation and cleanup operations run locally in your browser. The extension never collects, monitors, transmits, or logs browsing history, cookies, or personal data.
No Analytics or Network Calls: The extension makes zero remote network requests and contains no tracking libraries.
Storage Usage: Chrome sync storage is used exclusively to store the user-defined whitelist.
No Data Monetization: No user data is ever collected, sold, or shared.
